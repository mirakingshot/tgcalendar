    Week by week

    Weekly cap is 100 refinements: the Monday batch plus 1 per day Tuesday to Sunday. The first refinement each day costs half. Costs: 20/50/100/130/160 TG per refinement across each block of 20. Average TTG per refinement is estimated from the planner’s strategy table, so treat averages as close approximations.
